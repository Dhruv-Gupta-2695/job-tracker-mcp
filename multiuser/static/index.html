NextStep

Stay on top of every application.

Your job search, organised from your inbox

NextStep reads your application emails, keeps every company and status in one place, and helps you tailor your CV and reply to recruiters.

NextStep is in open beta. When you connect Gmail, Google may show an “unverified app” screen. Choose Advanced, then Go to NextStep to continue.

How it works

  1. 1

    Connect Gmail

    Sign in, then allow read-only access to your inbox.

  2. 2

    We scan and sort

    Applications, interviews, offers and rejections are picked out and tracked for you.

  3. 3

    Tailor and reply

    Generate a CV and cover letter per job, and draft replies that you review before anything is sent.

Why Google sign-in only?

Your applications live in Gmail

Confirmations, interview invites and rejections arrive in your inbox, so the account you sign in with is the one we read. There is nothing to import by hand.

Why Google sign-in only?

Read-only by default

We can only read emails until you choose Allow replies later, and nothing is ever sent without you reviewing and confirming it.

Why Google sign-in only?

Private to you

Your data is stored under your account only, and you can delete all of it at any time.

Other settings

      

        Email replies
        
Lets you write a reply with AI and save it to your Gmail Drafts (or send it, if the site owner has turned sending on). This asks Google for one extra permission, to create drafts and send mail. You can turn it off any time here, and remove the permission at your Google permissions page.

        

      

      

        Delete my data
        
Permanently deletes your applications, CV, generated documents, saved key and scan history from this site. It cannot be undone. Your Google account and Gmail are not touched. To also remove Gmail access, use your Google permissions page.
